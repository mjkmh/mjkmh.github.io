Biography

He is a faculty member at the Department of Geological Engineering, Faculty of Mines, Istanbul Technical University. His research focuses on engineering geology, rock mechanics and spatial analysis in the earth sciences. Combining field observations, laboratory experiments, numerical analyses and spatial data processing, he studies the engineering behaviour of rock and soil, geological hazards, and the impact of these hazards on infrastructure systems, settlements and cultural heritage structures.
